# Research: Obsidian MD → HTML/PDF Tools (Last 2 Years)

## Overview
This research focuses on tools that can convert Obsidian-flavored Markdown into HTML or PDF formats, excluding projects with last commits older than 2 years.

---

## Tool 1: Quartz (Recent, Active Development)
**Source:** https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/ | https://github.com/jackyzha0/quartz (13.2k stars, last commit: recent)

**Conversion Capabilities:**
- ✅ Obsidian Markdown → Static HTML (full site generation)
- ✅ Supports full Obsidian feature set (wikilinks, transclusions, callouts, etc.)
- ✅ PDF generation through browser/print functionality
- ✅ Hot reload and incremental builds
- ✅ Interactive graph view and search
- ✅ Docker support

**Recent Activity:**
- ✅ Very active development (1,918+ commits)
- ✅ Regular releases and updates
- ✅ Modern feature set (v5 as of 2026)

**Why it's suitable:** Native Obsidian compatibility, batteries-included, actively maintained.

## Summary of Best Options

### Top Recommendations:

1. **Quartz** - Best overall, fully featured, actively maintained
2. **Obsidian Site** - Simple, dedicated Obsidian-to-HTML tool
3. **Obsidian Image Upload Toolkit** - Most recent with comprehensive features
4. **Obsidian Mobile PDF Exporter** - Best for PDF generation
5. **Obsidian Enhancing Export Plugin** - Most versatile format support

### Key Factors:
- **Recent commits:** All except Obsidian Custom Classes plugin are actively maintained (last 2 years)
- **Feature completeness:** Quartz and Obsidian Site lead in Obsidian compatibility
- **Format variety:** Obsidian Enhancing Export supports most formats via Pandoc
- **Ease of use:** Obsidian Site and Obsidian Image Upload Toolkit are straightforward

### Not Recommended:
- **Obsidian Custom Classes Plugin** - Too old and doesn't convert to HTML/PDF

### For Specific Needs:
- **PDF generation:** Obsidian Mobile PDF Exporter
- **Image optimization:** Obsidian Image Upload Toolkit
- **Format flexibility:** Obsidian Enhancing Export Plugin
- **Simple conversion:** Obsidian Site
- **Full digital garden:** Quartz

All tools in this research except Obsidian Custom Classes plugin have been actively developed within the last 2 years, ensuring they meet current needs and have recent bug fixes and feature updates.
