# Target Job Sites — Hungary (Software Dev)

Ranked by relevance for a developer job search in Hungary. Traffic data from SimilarWeb (June 2026).

---

## 🥇 Tier 1 — Must Use

### 1. Profession.hu
Hungary's #1 job portal by far — 7M+ monthly page views, 2000+ active IT listings. Most Hungarian employers (and multinationals with Hungarian offices) post here first. English-language filters available. If you only use one Hungarian site, it's this one.
🔗 https://www.profession.hu

### 2. LinkedIn
The default for multinational and English-speaking dev roles in Budapest. 300+ software engineer listings in Budapest alone. Set "Open to Work" (recruiters only), enable job alerts, and apply directly. Many companies here hire faster than on local boards.
🔗 https://www.linkedin.com/jobs

### 3. No Fluff Jobs
IT-focused job board with mandatory salary transparency — you see the pay range before applying. Strong presence in Hungary and Central Europe. Filters for remote/hybrid, tech stack, and experience level. Saves you from "competitive salary"黑洞.
🔗 https://nofluffjobs.com/budapest

---

## 🥈 Tier 2 — Strong Alternatives

### 4. CVonline.hu
Hungary's #2 job board — 4M+ monthly page views. Regional focus with city-specific portals. Good for reaching employers outside Budapest (Debrecen, Szeged, Pécs). Bilingual (HU/EN).
🔗 https://www.cvonline.hu

### 5. Wellfound (formerly AngelList)
Startup-focused. 144+ Hungary-based listings. Best for finding early-stage or Series A-C startups that move fast on hiring. Direct founder contact possible. If you want startup speed, start here.
🔗 https://wellfound.com/location/hungary

### 6. DevRadar
Hungarian dev-specific platform with salary data, tech stack filtering, and an interactive map. Smaller pool but highly targeted — every listing is tech. Salary ranges are mandatory on postings.
🔗 https://devradar.hu/en/

---

## 🥉 Tier 3 — Worth Checking

### 7. Jooble
Job aggregator — pulls from multiple Hungarian and international sources. 3M+ monthly visits. Good for cast-net searching without checking 10 sites manually. Same listing may appear from different sources.
🔗 https://jooble.org

### 8. Indeed.com
Global aggregator, #4 in Hungary. Useful for English-language searches and remote roles. Has company reviews and salary estimates. Cast a wider net.
🔗 https://www.indeed.com

### 9. Built In
Tech-focused job board with a Budapest/Hungary section. Lists startups and established tech companies. Good for seeing who's hiring in the ecosystem.
🔗 https://builtin.com/jobs/eu/hungary/budapest

### 10. Who is Hiring
Aggregates dev-focused job posts (HN-style). Currently ~1,950 Hungary jobs parsed. Smaller but curated — worth a weekly check.
🔗 https://whoishiring.hu/en/about

---

## 📌 Tier 4 — Supplementary

### 11. DreamJobs
Hungarian platform with developer-friendly filtering. Smaller volume but clean UX. Good for discovering companies you haven't heard of.
🔗 https://dreamjobs.hu

### 12. Google Jobs
Search "software engineer Budapest" on Google — job results appear directly in search. Aggregates from multiple sources. Not a standalone strategy, but a useful shortcut.
🔗 https://www.google.com/search?q=software+engineer+jobs+budapest

### 13. Recruitment Agencies (IT Desk)
- **Hays Hungary** — specialist IT recruitment
- **Randstad Hungary** — 368 open positions, offices in 6 cities
- **Manpower Hungary** — IT and specialist recruitment
- **Reed Hungary** — specialist recruitment, tech focus

Agencies can be useful for senior roles or hard-to-fill positions. They approach you, not the other way around.

---

## 💡 Tips

- **Set alerts on Profession.hu, LinkedIn, and No Fluff Jobs** — those three cover 80% of the market
- **Check company career pages directly** — some roles never hit job boards
- **Apply within 48 hours of posting** — response rates drop significantly after 2 weeks
- **Use Hungarian + English search terms** — some roles are posted only in one language
